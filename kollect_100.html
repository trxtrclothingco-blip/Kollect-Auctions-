Shipping

Shipping is covered and reimbursed by Kollect100 to the seller. All Items must be sent as sighned for and with a tracking number to be eligible for shipping fee Reimbursement. We do not store any credit/debit card information, and payments are arranged directly with the Buyer with a Kollect100 Sales Manager after the auction ends. Failure to complete payment will result in being blacklisted from this site and the sites of our partners. By bidding, you acknowledge that you understand these terms and agree to comply.
